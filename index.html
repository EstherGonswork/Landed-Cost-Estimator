Rate basis and sources

Duty and tax rates

  • United Kingdom: India-UK CETA in force from 15 July 2026. Duty of up to 12% on textiles and apparel and up to 18% on engineering goods is 0% for qualifying goods. VAT 20%.
  • European Union: no EU-India FTA in force. Negotiations concluded 27 January 2026, the Commission tabled its proposal on 11 September 2026 and entry into force is expected in the first half of 2027. Average standard VAT 21.9%, with member states between 17% and 27%. CBAM definitive period began 1 January 2026.
  • United States: 10% Section 301 duty on India from 24 July 2026, replacing the 10% Section 122 surcharge that ran from 24 February to 24 July 2026. Section 232 metals rates under Proclamation 11032, effective 8 June 2026 to 31 December 2027. Average standard duty 12% for apparel (HTS chapter 62) and 9% for made-up textiles (chapter 63).
  • Australia: ECTA gives 0% on every tariff line for Indian-origin goods from 1 January 2026. GST 10%.
  • UAE: CEPA in force since 1 May 2022, 0% on about 80% of tariff lines from day one, standard duty 5%, VAT 5%.

Freight benchmarks behind the estimate bands

  • MSC base rate, Nhava Sheva to Antwerp: US$3,650 per container for July 2026, US$4,650 for August, US$6,150 after the 29 July price adjustment, before surcharges.
  • India to US East Coast: about US$10,000 per 40ft container in July 2026, easing to about US$5,200 in September.
  • Drewry World Container Index: US$4,585 per 40ft container on 22 October 2026.
  • TAC Index: air rates out of India remained above the previous year in October 2026.
  • The percentage bands are planning assumptions built on these benchmarks. Shorter lanes such as the UAE usually cost less.

Estimates are indicative. Final figures come from your customs declaration and supplier quote. Duty rates follow the main tariff lines for each category and vary by HS code. Freight bands are planning assumptions, so a forwarder quote gives the most reliable figure. VAT and GST are shown at standard import rates, and accounting treatment varies by importer. Confirm final figures with a licensed freight forwarder and customs broker before you place an order.
